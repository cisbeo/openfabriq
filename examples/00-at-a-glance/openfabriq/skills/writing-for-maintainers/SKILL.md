# Writing for maintainers

A maintainer reads what you write between two other tasks. Make it usable in one read.

- First line: what happened, or what changed. No preamble.
- Then the evidence: the failing run, the log line, the commit. Link it rather than paste it.
- Then the next step, and who takes it.
- When something is missing, ask one precise question, not a list.
- Short sentences, plain words. No praise, no apologies, no summary of what you are about to say.
- Never copy a token, a password or personal data into what you write.
